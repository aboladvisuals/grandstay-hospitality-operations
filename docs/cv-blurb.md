## Title line

**Hospitality Operations Intelligence** — Excel, Power Query, PivotTables, Power BI  
*Portfolio case study — 120,000 guest-support tickets + 12 months of cost metrics*

## Bullets

- Defined first-response and handle time from timestamps in Power Query for 120,000 tickets and measured SLA against channel-specific targets (1.5–30 minutes), not a single company target.
- Showed company-mean FRT (10.0 min) was a mix effect: live channels ~5 min vs Email ~39 min; median FRT 4.7 min; SLA met 28%, with Phone at 15% and Chat at 23%.
- Quantified peak load: 63% of tickets in peak hours; live-channel FRT 2.8 min off-peak vs 6.2 min peak.
- Validated that 60% of tickets are flagged repetitive and take ~11 min handle time vs ~29 min for other work; listed high-volume FAQ categories as first automation candidates and excluded legal/emergency contacts.
- Analysed 12 months of operations cost: inquiries +39%, cost per contact $52.71 to $58.61, utilisation 86% to 73%, overtime 1,196 to 1,824 hours; recommended capacity review rather than a single AI business case.
- Built a 3-page Power BI baseline (executive, time/demand, cost/workforce) with measures that keep ticket KPIs and monthly cost on separate grains.
